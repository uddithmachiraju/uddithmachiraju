Machine Learning is when you summon a data demon from the Spreadsheet Realm, feed it 1,000 pictures of spaghetti, and whisper:

**“Tell me what cats look like.”**

And it responds:

**“Spaghetti… is cat.”**

Then you say, “No, spaghetti is food,” and it goes,

**“Ah yes. Food = cat. Cat = toaster. I understand now.”**

It’s not coding. It’s not math.  
It’s <em>✨vibes-based intelligence✨</em>.

You're not building an algorithm — you're raising a confused robot that learns to recognize faces by accidentally studying ceilings.

One day it’s sorting apples.  
The next day it thinks your uncle is a watermelon.  
And we call this… progress.
